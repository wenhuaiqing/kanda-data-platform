# POL-04 Funding periods and budgets

Synthetic policy written for this demonstration.

## 1. Funding period
Every service falls inside the client's funding period: their NDIS plan, or their Support at Home agreement. A service outside it is held until the new plan or agreement is loaded into the CRM, then claimed against it. It is not claimed against the old one.

## 2. Remaining budget
A claim never exceeds what is left of the client's budget. When a service would take the client over, it is held and routed to the plan review with the coordinator, who agrees with the client and their plan manager what happens to services already delivered and to the roster from here.

## 3. Plan renewals
A renewal approved by the funder but not yet in the CRM is not funding the platform can see. The coordinator loads it; the held services then reprocess. Until then, the hold stands.
