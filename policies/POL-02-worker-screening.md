# POL-02 Worker screening and compliance

Synthetic policy written for this demonstration.

## 1. Screening is a condition of rostering
Every support worker holds a current worker screening clearance. The HR system records its expiry date. A worker whose clearance has expired is not rostered.

## 2. Services after expiry
A service delivered by a worker after their screening expired is held and escalated to the compliance team the same day. It is not claimed until compliance has reviewed it, whatever the worker or a coordinator says about a renewal in progress. A renewal counts when the HR system shows it.

## 3. One place at a time
A worker cannot deliver two services at once. Check-ins at two clients that overlap by more than 15 minutes are held and escalated to the coordinator, who establishes which visit happened and when. Shared or group supports are rostered as one service with a group item, not as overlapping individual services.
