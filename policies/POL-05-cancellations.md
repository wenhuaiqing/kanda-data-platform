# POL-05 Cancellations

Synthetic policy written for this demonstration.

## 1. Short notice cancellations
A service the client cancels with less than 7 days' notice is claimable as a short notice cancellation, at the rostered time and rate. The cancellation must be recorded - in the roster, or in the CRM contact log with the time it was received.

## 2. Cancellations with notice
A service cancelled with 7 days' notice or more is not claimed.

## 3. A cancellation the roster does not show
When the CRM contact log records a short notice cancellation that the roster still shows as scheduled, the cancellation stands: the roster is corrected and the service is claimed as a short notice cancellation, not cancelled as a no-show.
