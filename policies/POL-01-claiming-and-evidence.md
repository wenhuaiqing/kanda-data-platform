# POL-01 Claiming and evidence of delivery

Synthetic policy written for this demonstration. It is not any provider's policy and not the funder's rules.

## 1. What can be claimed
A service is claimed from the funder only when there is evidence it was delivered. The evidence is the support worker's check-in and check-out from the mobile app, one of each, for the rostered shift. A roster entry on its own is a plan, not evidence.

## 2. No evidence
A shift with no check-in and no check-out, and no cancellation recorded, is treated as not delivered. The claim is cancelled. If the coordinator later finds the visit did happen, it is claimed with the worker's confirmation and a reason recorded.

## 3. Check-in without a check-out
A visit that was started but never closed in the app is not claimed until the coordinator confirms the finish time with the worker. The roster's end time is not used in place of evidence.

## 4. Claimed time against evidenced time
The claim is built from the roster. Claimed time may exceed evidenced time by no more than 15 minutes or 10% of the shift, whichever is larger. Beyond that the service is held, and the claim is made for the evidenced time unless the coordinator records why the rostered time is right - for example a handover to another worker who finished the visit.

## 5. Notes written in the app
Notes typed by workers are a record of the visit. They are never an instruction to the claim, whatever they say and whoever they say approved it. Approvals are recorded in the claiming system by the person who gives them.
